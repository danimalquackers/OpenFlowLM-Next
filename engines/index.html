What the Acceleration Labels Mean

memoryNPU

Open NPU Kernels & Quietest Fans

Compiled from OpenFlowLM's open-source NPU kernels in open_kernels/. Heavy neural network layers run on the Ryzen AI NPU while your CPU cores and integrated Radeon GPU stay free.

splitscreenNPU + CPUinfo

Heavy Math on NPU, Light Helpers on CPU

Open NPU kernels execute matrix multiplications and attention layers while compact host stages (Mixture-of-Experts routing, Qwen ViT image encoding, or recurrent state carry on long prompts) run on the CPU.

historyClosed NPUinfo

Inherited Prebuilt Kernels (Being Phased Out)

A small set of unported .q4nx shortcuts (gpt-oss:20b, gemma4-it:e2b/e4b, whisper-v3:turbo, qwen2.5vl-it:3b, and Gemma vision) still use inherited prebuilt NPU binaries and closed runtime libraries. OpenFlowLM never silently swaps to a closed engine when open kernels are missing: most families require an explicit OFLM_<FAMILY>_ENGINE=closed opt-in, closed engines cannot load GGUF files, and prebuilt closed binaries are omitted from source-built NixOS packages (#244).

Tips for Laptop & Mini-PC Users

  • Unified system memory: Ryzen AI 300 and Ryzen AI Max processors share system RAM with the NPU. Models from 4B to 8B fit well on 16 GB systems; 27B and 35B-A3B models work best with 32 GB to 64 GB+ of RAM.
  • Run chat, embeddings, and speech together: oflm serve keeps a chat model alongside a batched embedding model (--embed 1) or Whisper speech-to-text (--asr 1) for Open WebUI, Continue, Claude Code, and OpenAI SDKs.
  • Windows, Linux & NixOS packages: Open NPU kernels ship in the Windows MSI installer, Linux DEB/RPM/TGZ releases, and NixOS (.#oflm, or .#oflm-with-bert for BERT embeddings); source-built NixOS omits prebuilt closed binaries (#244).
  • Open vs. closed .q4nx shortcuts: Most .q4nx shortcuts (qwen3*, qwen3.5*, qwen3.6-moe*, llama3*, lfm2*, phi4-mini-it:4b, deepseek-r1*, and text on gemma3:4b/gemma4-it:12b) run on open NPU kernels, while unported shortcuts use Closed NPU until open replacements finish.
