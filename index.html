Local Image Generation & Editing

Create 512x512 and 1024x1024 images or restyle reference photos offline with FLUX.2 [klein] 4B on your NPU.

oflm image flux2-klein:4b \
  "a red fox in fresh snow" -o fox.png
oflm image flux2-klein:4b \
  "Make it watercolor" --image fox.png
